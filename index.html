Privacy Policy

Purr Memo · Last updated: October 11, 2026

📄 Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Purr Memo ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our iOS application. We take your privacy seriously and have built the App to work without collecting any personal data.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

🚫 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
  • Your location (GPS or otherwise)
  • Your device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, or advertising IDs)
  • Your photos, memos, or any content you create in the App
  • Usage analytics, crash logs, or behavioral data
  • Any other personal or identifiable information

The App does not connect to any server we operate. There is no backend, no cloud sync, and no user account system.

📱 Data Stored On Your Device

All information you create in the App is stored only on your device and never leaves it unless you explicitly choose to share it. This includes:

  • Your memo text
  • Your selected cat template, font, and colors
  • Your chosen stickers and photo mask
  • Your signature name
  • Your memo photo (stored in the App's private Documents folder)
  • Your app preferences (stored in UserDefaults)

If you delete the App, this local data is permanently removed from your device. We have no copy and cannot recover it for you.

🖼️ Photo Library Access

To add a photo to your memo, the App uses Apple's built-in photo picker (PHPicker). This picker runs outside the App and shares only the specific photo you select — the App has no access to the rest of your photo library.

When you save your finished memo image to your Photos app (via the share sheet's "Save Image" option), iOS asks for Add Only permission the first time. You can revoke this at any time in Settings → Purr Memo → Photos on your iPhone.

The App does not read, scan, upload, or analyze any of your photos. Photos you choose are used only to render your memo and are stored locally within the App's private folder.

🛒 In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to unlock all cat templates. All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive, see, or store your payment information — Apple handles the entire transaction.

Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID and can be restored at any time by tapping "Restore Purchases" in the App.

🔗 Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or social-media SDKs. The only third-party service involved is Apple's App Store (for in-app purchases), which operates under Apple's Privacy Policy.

🧒 Children's Privacy

Purr Memo is safe for users of all ages, including children. Because we do not collect any personal information, the App complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 — or from anyone else.

🔒 Data Security

Since all data stays on your device and we operate no servers, the security of your information depends on your device's own protections (passcode, Face ID / Touch ID, and iOS security). We recommend keeping your device updated and using a passcode.

✅ Your Rights

Because we do not collect any personal data, there is nothing for us to delete, export, or correct on your behalf. You have full control over all your data directly on your device:

  • Remove a photo: Long press the photo in the App's Settings to remove it.
  • Delete all app data: Delete the App from your device. This also removes your memo text, settings, and any saved photo.

📝 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, if we add a new feature that requires disclosure. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
