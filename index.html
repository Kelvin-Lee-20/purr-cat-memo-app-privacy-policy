Privacy Policy

Purr Memo · Last updated: October 12, 2026

📄 Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Purr Memo ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. Your privacy is our top priority.

The App is designed to work entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. There are no user accounts and no registration requirements.

🛒 In-App Purchases and Payment Processing

The App offers an optional one-time In-App Purchase to unlock all cat templates. All transactions, payments, and receipt verifications are processed entirely and securely by Apple through the App Store infrastructure. We do not collect, store, or have access to your financial information, credit card numbers, or billing details.

Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID and can be restored at any time by tapping "Restore Purchases" in the App.

📱 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To function properly, the App stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Your memo text and signature name
  • Your selected cat template, font, size, and color
  • Your chosen stickers and photo mask
  • Your memo photo (stored in the App's private Documents folder)
  • Your app preferences

This data is stored securely using Apple's standard technologies. Neither we nor any third party can access this data. If you delete the App, this data is permanently deleted.

🖼️ Photo Library Access

To add a photo to your memo, the App uses Apple's built-in photo picker (PHPicker). This picker runs outside the App and shares only the specific photo you select — the App has no access to the rest of your photo library.

When you save your finished memo to your Photos app, iOS asks for Add Only permission the first time. You can revoke this at any time in Settings → Purr Memo → Photos on your iPhone.

🔗 Third-Party Services and Data Collection

Apart from standard App Store transaction services provided by Apple, the App does not use any third-party services that collect data. Specifically, we do not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • Crash reporting services
  • Social media integrations
  • Cloud synchronization

🧒 Children's Privacy

The App is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal data, there are no privacy risks for children.

📝 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page when changes are made. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
